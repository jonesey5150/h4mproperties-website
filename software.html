SOFTWARE & APPS

Practical software.
No unnecessary nonsense.

H4M Properties, LLC develops straightforward software designed to solve everyday problems. Our approach is simple: build useful tools, keep them easy to use, and don't turn features that should be simple into complicated services.

OUR APPROACH

Software should serve the person using it.

We believe software should serve the person using it—not the other way around. That means respecting privacy, avoiding unnecessary data collection, and being thoughtful about when a product actually needs an account, subscription, or ongoing fee.
