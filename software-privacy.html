SOFTWARE PRIVACY

Your privacy belongs to you.

Effective Date: October 13, 2026

At H4M Properties LLC, we believe software should serve its customers without collecting or exploiting their personal information.

Our Privacy Commitments

Software products covered by this policy:

  • Do not collect personal information.
  • Do not track users or their activities.
  • Do not use advertising trackers or analytics.
  • Do not sell, share, or monetize user information.
  • Do not require accounts or registration.
  • Do not transmit personal information to H4M Properties LLC.

Information Stored on Your Device

Some applications may save preferences, settings, or information entered by users directly on their devices. This information is not collected by H4M Properties LLC.

Third-Party Services

Our covered software does not incorporate third-party advertising or tracking services. Purchases and downloads through application marketplaces may be subject to those providers' separate privacy policies.

Future Software Products

This policy covers H4M Properties LLC software products that follow the practices described here. If a future product uses different data practices, we will disclose those practices in an updated or product-specific policy before release.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy as our software products and applicable requirements evolve.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to H4M Properties LLC through our contact page.
